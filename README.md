\# Sales Data Analysis Project



\## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales transaction data using SQL and Microsoft Excel to identify product performance, revenue trends, and profitability.



\## Objectives

\- Analyze total revenue and profit.

\- Compare sales performance across products.

\- Identify monthly revenue trends.

\- Build a dashboard to communicate key business metrics.



\## Tools and Technologies

\- \*\*SQL / MySQL:\*\* Querying and analyzing sales data.

\- \*\*Microsoft Excel:\*\* Data analysis, KPI calculations, and dashboard visualization.

\- \*\*Git and GitHub:\*\* Version control and project sharing.



\## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Result |

|---|---:|

| Total Revenue | ₹4,86,900 |

| Total Profit | ₹1,25,500 |

| Total Units Sold | 48 |

| Total Orders | 12 |

| Profit Margin | 25.77% |



\## Key Findings

\- March recorded the highest monthly revenue at ₹1,92,400.

\- Laptops generated the highest revenue at ₹2,75,000.

\- Laptops also generated the highest profit at ₹65,000.

\- The overall profit margin was approximately 25.77%.



\## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard includes:

\- Revenue by Product

\- Profit by Product

\- Monthly Revenue Trend

\- Key performance indicators



\## Project Files

\- `sales.csv` — Sales transaction dataset.
